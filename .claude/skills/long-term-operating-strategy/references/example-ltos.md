**Meridian Coffee Roasters**

Long-Term Operating Strategy — Example

---

## About This Document

This document translates the Constitution and Brand Manifesto into a strategic operating plan. Where those documents are permanent and foundational, this document is situational and time-bound. It will be updated at each annual planning cycle as the business grows, channels mature, and priorities shift.

## Situation

### The Market

Specialty coffee consumption in Northern Europe grew at a compound annual rate of roughly 8% between 2022 and 2025, driven by consumers seeking traceable sourcing and single-origin transparency over commodity blends. The roasters positioned to capture that demand are almost entirely direct-to-consumer subscription brands with limited retail presence. The window for a founder-led, provenance-first roaster to establish a defensible wholesale and specialty-retail position is open, but large-scale entrants are beginning to notice the same data.

### The Business

Meridian Coffee Roasters began as a single roastery serving three local cafes and has grown, through the founder's direct relationships with growers in two countries, into a small but genuinely distinct operation: green coffee sourced through documented, visited relationships rather than broker lists. Today the business generates approximately €180,000 in annual revenue across wholesale accounts, a small e-commerce operation, and a monthly farmers market presence. The sourcing relationships are the business's most defensible asset. The commercial infrastructure around them, marketing, wholesale sales process, e-commerce operations, is largely undeveloped.

## Complication

### The Story Is Undocumented

What makes Meridian distinct, the grower relationships, the sourcing trips, the roast development process, exists almost entirely in the founder's head and in scattered social posts. A wholesale buyer or retail customer encountering the brand for the first time has no consistent way to understand what separates it from any other specialty roaster.

## Stakes

The specialty coffee category is consolidating faster than most founders expect. Regional roasters with strong sourcing stories are increasingly acquisition targets for larger players looking to buy authenticity rather than build it. The window to establish an independent, recognized position is open now, and it narrows every year the story stays undocumented.

## Vision

Meridian Coffee Roasters becomes the reference point for transparently sourced coffee in its home region, the name wholesale buyers and serious home brewers bring up unprompted. By the end of the plan horizon, wholesale accounts and e-commerce subscriptions are the primary revenue engines, the founder's role shifts from doing every roast personally to overseeing quality and sourcing, and the brand's story is documented well enough that it survives being told by someone other than the founder.

## Objectives

Three objectives must be achieved in sequence before the vision is reached.

First: document the story and stabilize wholesale. By the end of year one, the sourcing narrative exists in a form a new hire or a wholesale buyer can absorb without the founder present, and the wholesale channel operates on a repeatable process rather than personal relationships alone.

Second: build the subscription engine. In years two and three, e-commerce subscription becomes a structural revenue stream, not an occasional online order, supported by content that makes the sourcing story visible at the point of purchase.

Third: extend the sourcing relationships into new formats, tasting events, limited releases, direct grower visits customers can join, that deepen the brand's credibility rather than simply adding volume.

## Strategy

The strategy is to document and systematize before scaling. The sourcing relationships and the roasting expertise already exist; what has to be built is everything that makes them visible and repeatable without the founder in every interaction.

| Period | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theme | Document | Systematize | Extend |
| State | The sourcing story exists in a form others can tell. Wholesale runs on a repeatable process. | Subscription becomes a structural channel. Content makes the story visible at purchase. | New formats deepen credibility without diluting focus. |

### Document (Year 1)

The founder's knowledge gets written down, the grower relationships, the roast profiles, the reasons behind them. Wholesale moves from relationship-dependent to process-dependent, so a new account can be opened without the founder personally present for every conversation.
